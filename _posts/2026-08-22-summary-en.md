## [Largest 2D Map of the Universe Released by DESI Legacy Surveys](https://newscenter.lbl.gov/2026/08/10/scientists-release-biggest-2d-map-of-the-universe/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys team has released the largest-ever 2D map of the universe, comprising 5.6 trillion pixels and nearly 4 billion celestial objects, built from over 263,000 telescope exposures in visible and near-infrared light. This map provides an unprecedented view of the universe, covering roughly three-quarters of the sky, and serves as the foundation for the largest-ever 3D map of the universe, which will be used to investigate dark energy and advance cosmological research. The map is accessible via the Legacy Survey Sky Viewer at viewer.legacysurvey.org. It includes stars, galaxies, black holes, and asteroids, and is expected to remain the most comprehensive 2D map for years to come.

hackernews · NKosmatos · Aug 21, 18:36 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49392200)

**Background**: The DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys combine data from multiple surveys (MzLS, DECaLS, BASS) to create an inference model of the extragalactic sky in optical and infrared. This 2D map records the positions of celestial objects on the sky but not their distances, which are needed for a 3D map. The 3D map, built on this foundation, will help astronomers study dark energy and the large-scale structure of the universe.
